HOW THE GOLD LABELS WERE BUILT
------------------------------
- Law ids: picked by hand from the 81 family_law_2 documents (full list
  reviewed).
- Case ids: title keyword search over all 2,926 family_law posts, then
  hand-filtered (e.g. "residency/citizenship" posts were dropped from the
  right-to-reside topic).
- The generator checks every id exists in data/chunked/*.json and is in the
  correct bucket, and refuses to write the file otherwise.
- Gold lists mean "known relevant", NOT "the only relevant documents". The
  corpus has many on-topic posts per topic, so an on-topic result that is
  not in the gold list is not a miss.

LANGUAGE TWINS
--------------
Several topics appear as English + Hindi (or Tamil / Hinglish) versions of
the same question with the same gold labels: right to reside (en/hi/ta),
custody (en/hi), dowry (en/hi), mutual consent (en/hi), bigamy (en/hi).
Comparing twins shows whether translation makes retrieval language-
independent.

HOW TO SCORE
------------
Run with the production settings (ENABLE_QUERY_TRANSLATION as deployed).
Per row:
  1. law_recall@k   - share of gold_law ids in the returned law_or_act results
  2. case_hit@k     - did at least one gold_case id appear in the case
                      results (stricter: case_recall@k)
  3. LLM-judged relevance - for every returned chunk, ask the judge
                      "is this relevant to the query? (0/1)"; report
                      precision. This catches on-topic results that are
                      not in the gold list.
  4. twin agreement - overlap of returned parent_ids between language twins
Rows with an empty gold list (498A law, same-sex/legal-aid case, interfaith
child case) should be scored on judged relevance only.

CORPUS FINDINGS WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU SCORE
----------------------------------------------
- The DV Act IS in the corpus (parent_ids 2930 and 2952). When the earlier
  manual-test answer left out the right to reside (s.17), that was a
  ranking/generation problem, not missing data.
- There is no IPC/BNS or CrPC/BNSS statute document, so criminal-law
  questions (498A, bigamy punishment) can't retrieve a statute.
- Several separate articles cover the same ruling or act and take up law
  slots: 2962/2986/2987/2988 (Muslim women maintenance), 2989-2992 (child
  of Muslim-Hindu marriage), 2997/2998, 3000/3001, 3006/3007. They are not
  copies (text overlap is under 20%). corpus_curation.py now returns at
  most one document per subject.
- family_law_2 contains documents unrelated to family law, e.g. 3009 (tax
  incentives for FDI), 2980 (vehicle repossession), 2982 (fisheries act),
  2978 (bail order), 3010 (arrest grounds). They can occupy law slots.
- 64 chunks in Milvus (7 documents, category = filename, no parent record,
  no url) are not in data/chunked and are not covered by this dataset.
- Some corpus answers are outdated. For example, post 2845 ("Daughter in
  law's rights") says a wife needs the father-in-law's permission to live
  in his house, which conflicts with Satish Chander Ahuja v. Sneha Ahuja
  (2020). Retrieval can be "correct" and still feed the generator wrong
  law.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-----------------
- Gold case lists are based on titles; a reviewer should open a few posts
  to confirm their content matches.
- 36 queries are enough to compare before/after a change, not to estimate
  absolute recall precisely.
